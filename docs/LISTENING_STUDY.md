# Independent listening evidence: prepared, not completed

The rebuild establishes software and optimization correctness. It does not establish that acoustic affinity predicts liking, that its boundary costs sound smoother, or that people prefer automatic shared queues. No listener judgments were present in the input documents and none have been fabricated.

Run `python evaluation/study.py --output output/study` to generate blank song, transition, fixed-selection order and session instruments with randomized trial IDs and a private key. Add `--render-audio` to create local hard-cut excerpts from available music. Excerpts preserve audible leading/trailing silence and loudness changes; the renderer adds no fade, normalization or gap. Boundary RMS and silence measurements are logged separately. Keep the private prediction/method key away from raters. Do not redistribute music or excerpts without confirming the owner's distribution permissions.

The song instrument asks session acceptance, ordinary dislike, explicit do-not-play, familiarity, preference strength, and context. Unknown is a valid response. Participants use anonymous group/session/listener IDs and provide explicit consent. Incomplete blank rows remain unscored. Completed rows lacking consent or required identity/split fields are rejected. `python evaluation/study.py --evaluate output/study/song_judgments.csv` exports raw held-out comparisons for maximum, top-two and small-pocket predictors without inventing probabilities, significant effects or independent sample sizes.

Use non-seed recommendations for preference validity. For a new-track claim, keep duplicate and related recordings out of both tuning and test partitions. For a new-listener claim, hold listeners out of model/policy tuning; profile-forming seeds are not test outcomes. Freeze transforms and weights before test judgments, or explicitly declare a transductive protocol. Examine unequal profile sizes and separated taste pockets instead of choosing the best predictor on the same ratings used to report success.

For transition validity, randomize and blind the labels of actual rendered pairs. The fixed-selection order instrument compares pooled affinity, no sequencing and a simple measured loudness/silence baseline over exactly the same selected track IDs. Judge the order before attributing a joint selection-and-ordering gain to sequencing. The simple cost has stated diagnostic scales (20 dB and 2 seconds), not validated perceptual weights.

For shared-listening usefulness, elicit a real session's explicit acceptance and rules first. Compare round-robin favorites, shared approval, constraint-matched beam and MILP using the same eligible universe, evidence and hard promises. Do not let a baseline violate a rule and then count that as an optimizer quality gain. Log listener acceptance, worst-listener outcome, actual strongly unwanted exposure, actual waits, familiarity/discovery, transitions, skips, edits and curation effort. The session API tracks actual duration history only after playback is confirmed.

Begin with a usability pilot to test the instructions and estimate variability. Determine a later sample size from a meaningful effect and uncertainty; this repository makes no significance promise. Treat groups/sessions as the experimental unit for group conclusions. Counterbalance method order, familiarity and carryover in any randomized crossover.

Ablations have distinct purposes: full eligible ranking versus retrieval for recall; simple shared approval or round robin for aggregation; supplied favorites or metadata for the audio stage; fixed selection for ordering; identical objectives/constraints for solver comparisons. Keep a component for demonstrated listener value, computation, reliable enforcement or useful certificates. Disable or retire one that contributes none of those. No neural ranking, online bandits or distributed service was added without a measured need.

The R4 exit gate remains open until independent judgments exist. Production sharing and large-scale indexing are also gated on a chosen load/memory target and service controls. These are external evidence/deployment decisions, not work that the agent can truthfully manufacture.
