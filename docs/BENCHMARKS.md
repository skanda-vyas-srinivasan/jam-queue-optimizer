# Reproducible optimization evidence

`evaluation/audit/` preserves the supplied audit bundle unchanged. `evaluation/results/baseline-diagnostics.json` records the 24 checks rerun against actual upstream functions at reviewed commit `e637be5`. These intentionally assert the original faults. Do not run them as post-repair success tests. The independent ten-case HiGHS check is also saved; it is not a CBC reproduction or listening experiment.

`python evaluation/benchmark.py` writes complete immutable synthetic instances, raw CBC logs, repeated outcomes, wall times, priority differences and absolute affinity gaps. It compares greedy, deduplicated beam, exact subset DP, continuous-edge MILP, binary-edge MILP, constraint-matched round robin and shared approval. Every returned queue is independently validated. Five-track cases also have an exhaustive oracle. Infeasible and heuristic-no-solution outcomes remain in the published data.

The committed run contains 18 instances and 378 runs (three repetitions). Each exact method proved 51 feasible optima and the three repeated copies of the intentional joint conflict. DP and both edge formulations agreed. Beam's largest measured affinity gap was 0.110583; greedy's was 0.569077. These are differences in the chosen internal objective, not listening-quality gains. There is no universal runtime claim: cases are small, the hardware/software is recorded, startup and preprocessing scopes differ, and one formulation is not guaranteed faster.

`python evaluation/catalog_benchmark.py` measures six fully specified artificial rooms from the saved 121-song catalog, with shortlist limits 10 and 20 and two repetitions. The 96 committed runs include frozen instances, exact source track IDs, policy, model hash, construction timings and logs. All 24 DP and 24 MILP runs proved their candidate-scope optima under a two-second request budget. The maximum measured total was 1.919354 seconds in this run. Sampled seed labels are artificial input; these are not listener judgments and are not reproduction of the six PDF experiments whose generating scripts were not supplied.

Transition matrices are built only for the protected shortlist. The current 121-track retrieval matrix remains dense; no approximate index or distributed infrastructure was added. The local request target used here is at most two seconds for these 121-track artificial rooms at horizons five and candidate limits 10/20. This is a measured workbench target, not a production SLA. The server serializes solves, bounds input sizes and remains loopback-only. Shared production scaling is gated on a separately chosen load/memory target and production service controls.

Counterexample coverage lives in `tests/test_artifacts.py`, `tests/test_requests_candidates.py`, `tests/test_solvers.py`, `tests/test_debug_panel.py` and `tests/test_session.py`: reserved names/duplicate display names, empty profiles, duplicate/aliased seeds, shared coverage, minority retention, strict/joint caps, infeasible representation, partial and nondefault schemas, common transforms, frozen catalog growth, invalid decoding, directed/negative edges, nonzero penalty units, verified starts, short supply, zero/invalid sizes, timeout/gap status, locked anchors, preserved debt and stale-result protection. The last-index one-slot case expands only one surviving DP state, rather than scanning 524,289 subsets.

Property checks include renaming, alias deduplication, catalog permutation, deeply immutable coefficients, canonical score agreement and nested expansion under frozen rules. Separate fixed transition-endpoint fixtures show shortlist opportunity loss. The benchmark files are fresh experiments; the original report's elapsed times and enjoyment hypothesis have not been relabeled as independently reproduced findings.
